Privacy Policy

for LunaRae

Last Updated: June 2026

LunaRae ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your information. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected when you use the LunaRae mobile application ("App"), how that information is used, and the choices available to you.

By using LunaRae, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

Story Prompts and User Content

When you use LunaRae to generate a story, the text you enter is transmitted securely to our AI service provider, OpenAI, to generate a response.

Please do not include personal information in story prompts, including:

  • Real names
  • Home addresses
  • Email addresses
  • Phone numbers
  • Financial information
  • Sensitive personal information

LunaRae does not require user accounts and does not intentionally collect personally identifiable information through story prompts.

Analytics and Usage Information

We use Firebase Analytics to better understand how LunaRae is used and to improve the user experience.

Analytics information may include:

  • App launches
  • Screen views
  • Feature usage
  • Interaction events
  • General device information
  • Operating system version

This information is aggregated and used solely to improve the performance, reliability, and usability of the App.

Crash and Diagnostic Information

We use Firebase Crashlytics to monitor app stability and identify technical issues.

Crash reports may include:

  • Device model
  • Operating system version
  • App version
  • Error logs
  • Diagnostic information related to app crashes

This information helps us fix bugs and improve reliability.

Advertising Information

The free version of LunaRae displays advertisements through Google AdMob.

AdMob and its partners may collect certain information, including:

  • Device identifiers
  • Advertising identifiers
  • Approximate location information
  • Ad interaction data
  • Diagnostic information related to advertising services

On iOS devices, LunaRae may request permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) framework before allowing personalized advertising.

Users may choose whether to allow tracking when prompted by their device.

For more information about how Google uses data, please visit Google's privacy resources.

2. How We Use Information

Information collected through LunaRae is used to:

  • Generate AI-powered bedtime stories
  • Improve app performance and reliability
  • Monitor crashes and technical issues
  • Understand app usage patterns
  • Display advertisements in the free version
  • Maintain security and prevent abuse

We do not sell personal information.

We do not create user accounts.

We do not knowingly build personal profiles of users.

3. AI Story Generation

LunaRae uses technology provided by OpenAI to generate stories.

When you submit a story prompt:

  • The prompt is securely transmitted to OpenAI for processing.
  • OpenAI generates story content based on your prompt.
  • LunaRae does not use your prompts to train its own AI models.
  • LunaRae does not sell your prompts or story content.

LunaRae is an independent application and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by OpenAI.

For more information, please review OpenAI's Privacy Policy.

4. Children's Privacy

LunaRae is designed to provide family-friendly and child-friendly content.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Parents and guardians are encouraged to supervise use of the App and remind children not to enter personal information into story prompts.

If you believe a child has provided personal information through LunaRae, please contact us so that we can investigate and take appropriate action.

5. Data Storage and Security

We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect information processed through LunaRae.

Story prompts are transmitted securely for processing and are not intentionally stored by LunaRae beyond what is necessary to provide the service.

However, no internet-based service or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

6. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights under applicable privacy laws, including:

  • The right to request access to personal data
  • The right to request correction of inaccurate data
  • The right to request deletion of personal data
  • The right to object to certain processing activities
  • The right to withdraw consent where consent is required

Because LunaRae does not maintain user accounts and generally does not collect information that directly identifies users, our ability to identify or retrieve data associated with a specific individual may be limited.

7. Third-Party Services

LunaRae may use the following third-party services:

  • OpenAI (AI-powered story generation)
  • Google AdMob (advertising services)
  • Firebase Analytics (usage analytics)
  • Firebase Crashlytics (crash reporting and diagnostics)

These providers maintain their own privacy policies and practices.

8. App Tracking Transparency (iOS)

On supported iOS devices, LunaRae may request permission to track activity across apps and websites for advertising purposes using Apple's App Tracking Transparency framework.

Granting permission is optional.

Users may manage tracking permissions at any time through their device settings.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.

Any updates will be posted on this page and reflected by the "Last Updated" date above.

Continued use of LunaRae after changes become effective constitutes acceptance of the revised Privacy Policy.
